How name tags work
  • Craft a name tag from paper and an iron, gold or copper nugget, or find one in dungeon and mineshaft chests, while fishing, or for sale from a wandering trader.
  • Rename it in an anvil, which costs 1 level, then use it on the mob. The tag is used up, and a named mob no longer despawns (wandering traders still do).
  • The game’s own names are case sensitive: Toast works, toast doesn’t.
  • Actions & Stuff takes its names with a capital first letter or all in lower case.
  • Actions & Stuff is a resource pack, so its names only change looks. A mob that looks like a baby is still grown up, with a grown mob’s health, size and drops.
  • The pack also gives some looks to a few mobs at random. It picks again each time the mob loads, so name it to keep the look.
  • The pack’s names here are from Actions & Stuff 1.9, read from the pack itself.
